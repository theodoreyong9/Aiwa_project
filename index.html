Open a channel — a real handshake, QR-first, works fully offline

A channel needs your peer's real consent, not just a click on your side. Show the QR on screen, have your peer scan it with a camera — that alone is the "offline" transport, no network and no Bluetooth pairing needed (copy/paste and a share sheet work too). Once confirmed, the channel's own actions appear in the Actions tab, under "Session payment", "QR cash-out" and "Smart contract".

Start a channel with a peer

Show this QR to your peer (or send it any way you like) — it proves who you are and what you're proposing, and does nothing on its own until they accept it.

Accept an incoming channel request

Show this QR back to whoever requested the channel — nothing further needed on your side after that.

An open channel needs no root connection at all, for anything — it keeps working after Disconnect above.

Publish as yourself

Publishes a real, signed, addressable bundle (aiwa-platform's own real publish mechanism) — discoverable by its own address or by yours, real content-addressed dedup and version history included. Runs later in its own isolated, sandboxed context — never inside this wallet's own page, so it can never read your identity here.

Next: the YourMine sphere

Made from the version you just published (manifest ). The sphere holds no copy of the contract: it is pinned to that manifest, fetches the signed events, checks every signature and hash itself, and only then runs the code. It finds the events on this device (through a window it asks you to allow) or at the public address of an export, if you give one below. Read it, then send it to YourMine's submission field: nothing is submitted for you.

Publish via your channel

Publishes through this channel's own session identity — works fully disconnected too. Honest limit: discoverable by address, but "list by creator" for your root id won't find it (real, cryptographic authorship is the channel's session identity here) — see this app's README.
